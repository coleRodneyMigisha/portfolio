It started as a Spotify clone that looked right but played nothing. Now it streams full tracks from the Audius API, with search, genre playlists, favorites saved in your browser, seeking and a phone layout. There's no framework, build step or server, and a test drives real Chrome through the whole app.

The songs come from independent artists on Audius, so you won't find most mainstream names here. Those are licensed, and free APIs tend to stop at 30-second previews.
