## Why

I wanted something that kept a practical record of my work.

## What I did

I designed it and built it with Python, PySide6 and SQLite, with automated tests and a written record of my design decisions.

It handles daily tasks with subtasks, carries unfinished work forward automatically, keeps Sunday-based weekly objectives, runs a 25-minute focus timer, and borrows its accent colours from your desktop wallpaper.
