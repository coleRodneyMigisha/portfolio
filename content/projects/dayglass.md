## Why

I wanted something that kept a practical record of my work.

## What I did

I designed it and built it with Python, PySide6 and SQLite, with automated tests and recorded design decisions.

It has daily tasks with subtasks, automatic carry-forward of unfinished work, Sunday-based weekly objectives, a 25-minute focus timer, and accents taken from the desktop wallpaper.
