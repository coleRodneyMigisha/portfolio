About Me
I can't start anything without a reason. So step one is finding it, and step two is building the thing without losing sight of why it exists.

It all started with nosy questions. How does a TV broadcast end up in your living room? How does a bank know what's in your account? Animations, games, the internet: nearly everything I loved led back to a computer, and I wanted in. I couldn't pick just one corner of it, so I picked computer science.

These days I'm in my third year at Uganda Christian University, majoring in AI and Robotics with data science alongside it, because you can't perfect automation without understanding data. The plan is simple: start each year as a completely different person at everything I do.

How I work. I design the system and make the calls. AI tools write the first draft of the code, then I review it, change it and test it until it does exactly what I meant.
