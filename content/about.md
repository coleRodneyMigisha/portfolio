About Me
I have to have a reason before I start anything. So I look for the reason first, then build, making sure the reason is honoured.

It started as fascination. How does a television broadcast reach a room? How does a bank know what is in your account? Animations, games, the internet: so much of what I loved could be traced back to a computer, and I wanted to be able to say I was part of it. I could not pick one corner of it, so I chose computer science.

I am now in my third year at Uganda Christian University, majoring in AI and Robotics and also studying data science, because automation cannot be perfected without understanding data. My aim is simple: at the start of each year, to be a different person in everything I do.

How I work. I design my systems and make the decisions. I use AI tools to produce first drafts of code, then review, change and test them until they do what I intended.
