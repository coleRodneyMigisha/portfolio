## Why

Captions fall apart when rooms get noisy, and deaf and hard-of-hearing people pay the price. Noise can't touch lip movements, so they can keep captions usable when the audio gives up.

## My part

I'm the model lead. So far I've built the mouth cropping pipeline and the audio encoder, and refined the data on our GPU workstation.

## Where it stands

The data is verified and the preprocessing pipeline is tested. Training hasn't started, so there are no results to show yet.
