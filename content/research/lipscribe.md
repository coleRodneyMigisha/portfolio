## Why

Captions fail when rooms get noisy, and deaf and hard-of-hearing people pay for it. Lip movements are unaffected by noise, so they can keep captions usable when audio is degraded.

## My part

I am the model lead. So far I have built the mouth cropping pipeline and the audio encoder, and refined the data on our GPU workstation.

## Where it stands

The data is verified and the preprocessing pipeline is tested. Training has not started, so there are no results yet.
